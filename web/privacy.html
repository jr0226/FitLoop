1. Introduction & Overview

FitLoop ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and the controls you have over your data. FitLoop operates with strict privacy principles: we do not sell your personal information, we do not serve third-party ads, and we do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

To provide our fitness and nutritional tracking features, FitLoop processes the following categories of data:

A. Account & Authentication Information

  • Email Address & Password / Google Sign-In: We use Google Firebase Authentication to securely register, verify, and authenticate user accounts. Your passwords are encrypted and hashed by Firebase; we never see or store plain text passwords.
  • User Identifier (UID): A unique random string generated by Firebase to securely isolate and associate your personal data with your account.

B. Profile & Biometric Information

  • Personal Attributes: Age, biological sex, height, current body weight, target weight, fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced), available workout equipment, dietary preferences (e.g. Standard, Vegan, Vegetarian, Halal, Keto), and self-declared food allergies.
  • Purpose: Stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore under your unique account to calculate basal metabolic rate (BMR), daily caloric targets, and tailor exercise routine recommendations.

C. Workout & Exercise Data

  • Workout Logs & Routines: Completed exercises, sets, repetitions, weights lifted, workout duration, and estimated calories burned.
  • Favorite Exercises: Exercises you bookmark in the Exercise Library for quick workout assembly.
  • Purpose: Synchronized to your private Firestore account (`users/{uid}/workout_logs` and `users/{uid}/favorite_exercises`) to calculate volume progression, workout consistency streaks, and milestone achievements.

D. Nutrition & Meal Diary

  • Food Logs: Meal names, estimated portion sizes, calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats logged by date.
  • Purpose: Saved to Firestore (`users/{uid}/food_logs`) to display daily caloric intake and macro distribution against your personal goals.

E. Body Measurements

  • Measurement History: Weight checkpoints, body fat percentage, chest, waist, and hip circumferences entered voluntarily by the user.
  • Purpose: Stored in Firestore (`users/{uid}/measurements`) for visual progression graphs and exportable PDF health summaries.

3. AI Food Scanner & Image Processing Data Flow

Camera & Meal Photographs: Photos captured with FitLoop's AI Food Scanner are used solely for nutritional estimation.

When you take a photo of your meal:

  • Transmission: The compressed image is sent securely via TLS/HTTPS to our dedicated backend server hosted on Render (`/api/ai/analyze-food`).
  • AI Visual Recognition: The backend transmits the image in-memory to the Google Gemini API to identify the dish, estimate visible ingredients, and approximate portion grams and macros.
  • Zero Remote Storage of Photos: Our Render server processes photos in-memory and does not save or retain your images on remote disks or databases. No images are uploaded to Firebase Storage.
  • Local On-Device Caching: For fast offline loading and dairy review, a compressed copy is cached locally in your device's private sandboxed app folder (`food_images/.jpg`), and analysis JSON is cached in local `SharedPreferences`.
  • AI Safety Limitation: Computer vision estimates are approximate. Visual inspection cannot identify hidden seasonings, trace allergens, or verify religious/dietary certifications (e.g. Halal slaughter).

4. Android Health Connect Integration

FitLoop integrates with Android Health Connect to read activity data from your smartwatch or compatible fitness tracker.

  • Read-Only Access: FitLoop requests permission to read:
    • Steps (`READ_STEPS`)
    • Sleep duration and stages (`READ_SLEEP`)
    • Active calories burned (`READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED`)
    • Heart rate (`READ_HEART_RATE`)
    • Distance (`READ_DISTANCE`)
    • Exercise sessions (`READ_EXERCISE`)
  • No Write Back: FitLoop does not write or modify your records in Health Connect.
  • Strict Health Connect Policy Adherence: Health Connect data is displayed directly to you on the Home Dashboard. We never sell Health Connect data, never share it with third-party data brokers or advertising platforms, and do not use it for credit underwriting or insurance evaluation.
  • Revocation: You can revoke FitLoop's Health Connect permissions at any time directly in Android System Settings > Health Connect.

5. Local Notifications & Reminders

FitLoop provides optional daily reminders (Meal times, Workout reminder, Hydration reminder, and Sunday Weekly Summary). Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using Android's local notification manager. You have complete control: each reminder can be toggled on/off or rescheduled under Settings > Notifications & Reminders, and no notifications are sent if you opt out.

6. Advertising, Tracking & Third-Party SDKs

  • No Commercial Ads: FitLoop contains zero third-party advertisement SDKs (no Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, or Meta Audience Network).
  • No Behavioral Trackers: We do not track you across other applications.
  • Third-Party Infrastructure:
    • Google Cloud Firebase: Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Hosting, Database).
    • Render: API server proxy for AI recognition requests.
    • Google Gemini API: AI multimodal inference.

7. Data Security & Storage

All network traffic between FitLoop and our backend services uses secure TLS/HTTPS encryption. Firestore access rules strictly enforce ownership (`isOwner(userId)`), ensuring that only your authenticated account can read or modify your private records.

8. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You have full ownership of your data and can delete your account and all associated records at any time:

  • In-App Immediate Deletion: Navigate to Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your Firebase Auth record, your Firestore profile, all workout logs, custom routines, diet records, measurements, achievements, and clears local cached images.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit an account deletion request via our Data Deletion Request Page or by emailing our support address below.

9. Children's Privacy

FitLoop is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.
