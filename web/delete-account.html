How to Delete Your FitLoop Account & Data

FitLoop provides two convenient paths for deleting your account and associated personal information:

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

If you have the FitLoop app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open FitLoop and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap on Settings (gear icon in the bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation prompt and tap "Delete Everything".

Your account and all associated cloud data will be wiped immediately, and you will be returned to the login screen.

Option 2: Submit a Web/Email Request (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you may submit a deletion request directly to our privacy operations team:

What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

Upon receiving and confirming your deletion request, the following records are permanently wiped from our systems:

  • Firebase Authentication Record: Email address, login credentials, and user UID.
  • User Profile: Name, age, biological sex, height, weight, fitness goals, and dietary preferences (`users/{uid}`).
  • Food & Nutrition Logs: All meal diary entries and nutrient breakdowns (`users/{uid}/food_logs`).
  • Workout Logs & Routines: All completed workout sessions, sets, repetitions, and custom workout routines (`users/{uid}/workout_logs` & `users/{uid}/routines`).
  • Favorite Exercises: All bookmarked exercise items (`users/{uid}/favorite_exercises`).
  • Body Measurements: Historical weight and circumference checkpoints (`users/{uid}/measurements`).
  • Achievements: Earned badges and milestone progress (`users/{uid}/achievements`).
  • Local Cache: Local cached food thumbnail images and drafts on your device (`food_images/`).

Data Retention & Processing Timelines

  • In-App Deletion: Executed immediately (0 days retention). Cloud Firestore data and Firebase Authentication accounts are purged in real time.
  • Email Requests: Processed and verified within 7 business days of receipt. Once identity verification is confirmed, all associated records are permanently erased.
  • Retention Exceptions: None. FitLoop does not retain any fitness, dietary, or health metrics following account deletion. Any temporary server access logs (IP addresses for fraud prevention) are automatically rotated and purged after 30 days.
